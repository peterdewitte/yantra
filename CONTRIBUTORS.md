# Contributors

Yantra, with its radio parts (formerly yantra-radios, merged into this
repository, PLG-29), is written by "The Yantra contributors" (the
copyright holder named in every SPDX header). The radio parts were
started on 2026-11-03 by
[curtat.be](https://curtat.be) as a reference on building new programs
with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant; commits made with Claude carry a
`Co-Authored-By` trailer.

Contributions are welcome under the rules of
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). A contributor may add a line here
with the name they want to be known by (no e-mail address needed), and
may report hardware results in [docs/DEVICES.md](docs/DEVICES.md).

## Third-party authors

The bundled libraries keep their own authors and licences:
[THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md); the release's licence bundle
(`share/licenses/yantra-radios/BUNDLE.md`) carries their notices.
