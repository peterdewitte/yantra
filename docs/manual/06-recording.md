# 6 Recording and replay

Recordings keep every measurement record of a survey, so it can be replayed and analysed later.

## 6.1 Recording

Start and stop a recording on the Survey page. The ring buffer keeps the last minutes while no recording runs, so you can save what just happened.

## 6.2 Replay

A replay plays a recording back through the same processing as a live survey, at the speed you choose, with seeking.

## 6.3 Markers and voice notes

Markers note a moment of the survey with a label; voice notes record a short spoken note with the microphone.
