# 11 Post-processing

The Post page shows the measured points of a floor in different views.

## 11.1 Views of the points

Choose the value (level, C1 or quality of the serving cell, the strongest cell or a frequency), show it as values, as good and bad against a threshold, or with an offset, mark serving-cell changes and switch cells off to see the best remaining server.

## 11.2 Acceptance criteria

An acceptance criterion such as 95 % above -95 dBm is evaluated on the measured points, the tested squares or the interpolated floor area, and shows pass or fail.
